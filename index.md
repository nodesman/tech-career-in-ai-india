<section class="callout">
  <div class="callout-title">The Central Thesis</div>
  <p>For three decades, the Indian software career was subsidized by geographic labor arbitrage and the taxation of syntax: knowing where the semicolons went and how to wire corporate boilerplate was enough to purchase middle-class ascent. Generative AI and frontier reasoning engines have permanently erased that toll-booth.</p>
  <p>The survivor in this era is not the compliant ticket-closer or the LeetCode crammer, but the <strong>Sovereign Technologist</strong>—the high-acuity operator who absorbs commercial risk, commands autonomous model fleets, and creates indisputable value from first principles.</p>
</section>
